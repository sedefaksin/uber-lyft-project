# Uber vs Lyft: NYC High-Volume For-Hire Vehicle Trips (September 2026)

SQL Server analysis of ~21M NYC TLC High-Volume FHV trips (Uber = `HV0003`, Lyft = `HV0005`), comparing demand, wait time, pricing, geography and tipping.

## Key findings

- **Fare:** Uber's average fare is 5.6% higher ($28.95 vs $27.41), but about 4 points of that gap come from longer trips. City-wide price per mile is nearly the same ($5.73 vs $5.64, total fare / total miles).
- **Price per mile by borough:** the city-wide figure hides a split. Lyft is ~12-13% higher in Brooklyn, the Bronx and Staten Island, Uber is ~12% higher in Manhattan, and Queens is equal.
- **Demand by hour:** same daily rhythm for both: trough around 3 a.m., morning peak around 8 a.m., highest demand 17:00-19:59.
- **Weekday:** Uber's share is stable across the week (72.4% Sunday to 74.2% Monday); no clear weekday/weekend split.
- **Wait time:** typical Uber wait is shorter (median 4.57 vs 4.78 min) but its average is higher (5.74 vs 5.51), so Uber has a heavier right tail. Not driven by extreme values (trips over 1 hour are 0.007% of Uber trips). Uber is faster in Manhattan but slower in the other four boroughs, so geography does not explain the gap.
- **Geography:** both companies' top two pickup zones are LaGuardia and JFK. Uber is more Manhattan-centric (37.9% of pickups vs 31.3%); Lyft has larger shares in Brooklyn, Queens and the Bronx.
- **Airports:** at LaGuardia and JFK, Uber's average fare is 8-13% higher and its price per mile about 16% higher, even though its airport trips are slightly shorter.
- **Driver pay and tips:** driver pay is 78.3% of base fare at Uber and 75.8% at Lyft (a rough ratio, not a commission). Lyft riders tip more often (22.8% vs 17.6% of trips); among tipped trips the average amount is similar (~$6.7 vs ~$6.9). Cash tips are not recorded.

## Limitations

- Wait time covers driver assignment, arrival and boarding. 1.19% of trips were excluded from wait analysis because pickup was recorded before the request.
- Rejection rates differ by company (Uber 0.045%, Lyft 0.25%). Rejected Lyft rows are mostly zero-fare, Uber's mostly negative-fare and zero-mile. These look like real trips with broken fare fields; the share is small, but the cause is unknown.
- `base_passenger_fare` excludes taxes, tolls and some fees, so driver-pay ratio and airport fares are not total cost or commission.
- Results describe one month and do not explain *why* the companies differ.

## Reproduce

1. Download `fhvhv_tripdata_2026-09.parquet` from the [TLC trip record data page](https://www.nyc.gov/site/tlc/about/tlc-trip-record-data.page) into `C:\data\` (not committed, too large).
2. `python scripts/02_parquet_to_csv.py` to create `C:\data\trips.csv`.
3. Run the SQL files in `sql/` in order.

Requires SQL Server (Express works), Python 3 with `pyarrow` and `pandas`.
